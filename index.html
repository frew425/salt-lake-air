Sources

Utah DAQ monitors
Measured PM2.5 and ozone, now and for the past day. These are the regulatory instruments, the most accurate in the valley.
Utah DAQ forecast
The official daily call and burn restrictions, written by forecasters who know local inversions.
NOAA air quality forecast
Hourly PM2.5, ozone and smoke from the National Air Quality Forecast Capability, bias-corrected when available.
CAMS global model
A second-opinion hourly forecast via Open-Meteo. It also fills any hours NOAA is missing.
Mixing height and wind
NOAA GFS and HRRR weather models via Open-Meteo. Mixing height is how deep the layer of air is that pollution mixes into.

How the numbers are worked out

AQI. The big number is the US AQI for fine particles (PM2.5), using EPA's current breakpoints, where 0 to 9 µg/m³ is Good. It uses EPA's NowCast: a weighted average of the last 12 hours that leans on the most recent ones when levels are changing fast.

Hourly forecast. The next 24 hours use NOAA's hourly PM2.5 wherever it has a value, with the calibrated CAMS model filling gaps. Each hour is converted to AQI the same way.

CAMS calibration. CAMS is a coarse global model and often misreads the valley. Its PM2.5 is scaled by how it compared with the home monitor over the last 24 hours.

Trapped air. The strip under the charts shows mixing height. It gets darker as the layer gets shallower: under about 1,200 m, 700 m and 300 m. An inversion is called weak when the day's peak mixing height stays under 900 m and strong under 400 m.

Best time outside. The two-hour window with the lowest forecast PM2.5 that starts between 6 AM and 8 PM.

Smoke and ozone levels. Smoke is Light from 2 µg/m³, Moderate from 10 and Heavy from 35. Ozone is Moderate from 55 ppb, High from 71 and Very high from 86.

Updates. Data refreshes once an hour. If a source fails, the last good copy is kept while it still covers the hours ahead, and the failure is listed under Status.

A caution. Models tend to under-predict deep inversions. Trust Utah DAQ for whether it's a bad day, and the hourly lines for when.
